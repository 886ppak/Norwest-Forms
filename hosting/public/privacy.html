NCH Forms - Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

What the app does

NCH Forms helps crew members fill in timesheets and leave, and keep a job calendar. The app is run by its owner for a small group of workers.

Gmail access (optional)

If you choose Connect Gmail in the app, you give NCH Forms permission to read your Gmail messages, read-only (the Google scope gmail.readonly). This is used only to find mobilisation emails and turn the row with your own name into a job on your calendar.

  • Only the job rows that match your name are kept. Other people's names, phone numbers and card numbers in the same email are discarded.
  • Email text is read by the server, parsed, and then discarded. Full emails are not stored and are not sent to anyone else.
  • The app does not send, delete, label or change anything in your Gmail.
  • Your Google refresh token is encrypted on the server. It is used only to read your mail for the hourly import.
  • Google may ask you to connect again about once a week while the app is in testing mode. Your jobs stay on your calendar until you disconnect.

What is stored

  • Your sign-in email and the calendar data you enter in the app (jobs, roster, leave, timesheet details), which sync to your account so they appear on your other devices.
  • Jobs found from Gmail, as job rows only.
  • An encrypted Google access token, while you are connected.

Disconnecting and deleting

In the app, open your profile and choose Disconnect Gmail. This revokes the app's access at Google, deletes the stored token, and deletes every job that was imported from Gmail. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who can see your data

Your data is held in the app's Google Firebase project. The owner of the app can administer that project. Your data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Google user data

NCH Forms' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
