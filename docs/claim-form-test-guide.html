Try the new Training & Medical claim forms

Two new claim forms just went live on the Norwest Personnel side of the timesheet app. This walks you through filling one in and downloading the finished PDF, so you can compare it against the paper form before it's used for real.

Live app 886ppak.github.io/Norwest-Forms

Get to the forms

  1. 1
    Open the link above

    On a phone or a computer, either works. It opens straight to a company picker.

  2. 2
    Tap "Norwest Personnel"

    Not "Norwest Crane Hire" — the two new claim forms only appear on the Personnel side.

  3. 3
    Find the two new tabs along the top

    Alongside the usual Timesheet and Leave tabs, you'll now see:

    Training Claim Medical/D&A Claim

Test: Training Claim

Training Payment Claim Form
  1. 4
    Tap the "Training Claim" tab

    Fill in the fields with anything — this is just a test, nothing gets sent.

    Employee Name Position Type Employee No Training Course Name Training Requested by Training Date(s) Training Location Duration
  2. 5
    Tick all four declaration boxes

    Under "Employee Declaration". The form won't let you download until every box is ticked — that's deliberate, same as signing the paper form.

  3. 6
    Draw a signature

    In the signature box near the bottom — finger or mouse, anything counts.

  4. 7
    Tap "Download PDF"

    Not "Submit & Send" — that one also tries to open an email to hr@norwestpersonnel.com.au, which you don't need while testing. Download PDF just saves the file.

    Tip: the PDF lands wherever your browser normally saves downloads (usually a "Downloads" folder).

Test: Medical/D&A Claim

Medical & D&A Allowance Claim Form
  1. 8
    Tap the "Medical/D&A Claim" tab

    Fill in your details, then tick either "Periodic Medical Assessment" or "Drug & Alcohol (D&A) Test" and fill in its date and who organised it.

    Employee Name Position Type Employee No
  2. 9
    Tick all five declaration boxes

    Under "Employee Declaration" — same rule as the Training form, all five are required.

  3. 10
    Draw a signature, then tap "Download PDF"

    Same as the Training form — use Download PDF, not Submit & Send, while you're just testing it.

What to check in the downloaded PDF

  • It's a single page, and nothing is cut off or overlapping the logo.
  • Everything you typed appears in the right box.
  • The ticked declaration boxes show a checkmark.
  • Your signature shows up above the "Employee Signature" line.
  • There's a blank, greyed-out Office Use Only section near the bottom — that's for HR to fill in by hand after the form comes in, so it should be empty.
  • Overall layout — spacing, headings, table lines — looks close to the paper form you already use.
If anything looks off — a field missing, text cut off, wrong wording — flag it and it can be fixed before this goes out to everyone. Nothing you type into the test run is saved or sent anywhere unless you deliberately tap "Submit & Send".
